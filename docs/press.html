Short description

Fontes is a free, open-source (Apache 2.0) MCP server. It lets your AI app (Claude, Codex, Cursor, VS Code or any other app that runs local MCP servers) search court decisions and legislation live on court and government websites in 120+ jurisdictions and international courts. Every result names its source and links to the original; unofficial sources are flagged. It runs on your computer: no account, no database, no hosted service. It is a research tool, not legal advice.

Facts

Name
Fontes (Latin for "sources")
What
An MCP (Model Context Protocol) server: a plug-in that AI apps use to call outside tools
Coverage
124 jurisdictions and international courts: 108 UN member states, 4 other territories, 11 international courts and bodies, and the DIFC Courts. 72 are covered completely under a strict rule; the coverage page lists the gaps and why
Tools
Seven research tools (search case law, read a decision, search legislation, table of contents, one provision, full text, list jurisdictions), plus one to report a bug
Sources
Mostly official court and government websites and APIs, fetched live; the few non-government sources are flagged as unofficial
Languages
Documentation and installer in 80 languages
Price
Free. No account. France needs a free government API key
Licence
Apache 2.0
Status
Public beta, version 0.1.0 on PyPI. Sources are checked daily on the status page
Privacy
Sends the developer nothing by default; usage counts and bug reports are opt-in. Privacy notice
Made by
Yüce Karapazar, independently. No legal services are offered

Please don't write

  • "Covers 120+ countries": 12 of the 124 are international courts and bodies or the DIFC Courts, and 52 are covered only in part.
  • "Official sources" without "mostly", or "complete" or "every law".
  • That it gives legal advice. It is a research tool; read the original before relying on it.
