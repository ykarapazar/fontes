Cabo Verde: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Cabo Verde case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Tribunal Constitucional de Cabo Verde (tribunalconstitucional.cv): case law and legislation
  • Supremo Tribunal de Justiça de Cabo Verde, jurisprudência (stj.cv): case law
  • JURIS CV – Jurisprudência Cabo-verdiana (Conselho Superior da Magistratura Judicial): case law

What it covers

Supremo Tribunal de Justiça (~770 acórdãos, 1994-) and the two Relação courts from JURIS CV (CSMJ, HTML full text, non-commercial terms); Tribunal Constitucional decisions (acórdãos, pareceres, decisões sumárias; 1995-, court=tc) and the stj.cv selection (~360, 2021-, section=...) as PDFs; ~30 core laws (codes, court organic laws, constitutions) as hosted by the TC, not consolidated. The Boletim Oficial's terms forbid copying without INCV permission, so it is not used.

What it does not cover

  • Boletim Oficial (all laws and decrees beyond ~30 TC-hosted texts): INCV terms forbid copying 'sem a prévia autorização'
  • STJ: JURIS CV holds ~770 acórdãos (1994-2026) with thin years (e.g. 2014, 2016: 2 each; 1995-2006 almost none) and stj.cv ~360 (2021-); the rest of the STJ's judgments are not published online
  • First-instance and appeal court decisions: not researched yet
  • Consolidated legislation: not researched yet
