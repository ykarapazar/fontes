WTO dispute settlement (World Trade Organization): case law for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search WTO dispute settlement (World Trade Organization) case law at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • WTO dispute pages and dispute data files (www.wto.org): case law

What it covers

WTO disputes DS1 onwards. Search covers number, title, short title, parties, subject and agreements cited. get_decision('DS600') gives the Secretariat's summary of the dispute (incl. key panel/AB findings); report texts on docs.wto.org are not fetched (robots). The agreement texts themselves (GATT 1994, Anti-Dumping Agreement, SCM, TRIPS ...) are not covered: read them at https://www.wto.org/english/docs_e/legal_e/legal_e.htm.

What it does not cover

  • WTO agreement texts (GATT 1994, Anti-Dumping Agreement etc.): no legislation search; official texts at https://www.wto.org/english/docs_e/legal_e/legal_e.htm
  • Panel and Appellate Body report texts (docs.wto.org): robots.txt disallows ('Disallow: /')
  • Submissions, arbitration awards and other WT/DS documents: on docs.wto.org, robots.txt disallows
  • French and Spanish versions of the dispute pages: not built yet (English only)
  • GATT-era disputes before 1995: not researched yet
