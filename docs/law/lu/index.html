Luxembourg: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Luxembourg case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Legilux — Journal officiel du Grand-Duché de Luxembourg (Ministère d'État, Service central de législation): JOLux SPARQL + Akoma Ntoso XML: legislation
  • data.public.lu — Cour de cassation decisions (Administration judiciaire), yearly ZIPs of PDFs read member by member with HTTP Range: case law

What it covers

National legislation in French: consolidated codes and laws (~1,400) and the original Mémorial text of every other act. Law id = Legilux ELI, e.g. eli/etat/leg/code/civil or eli/etat/leg/loi/2018/08/01/a686. Case law: only the Cour de cassation, published PDFs 1971-today, listed by year/date/case number (no full-text search; see gaps).

What it does not cover

  • Cour de cassation full-text search: the source is yearly ZIPs of PDFs with no index, so search lists by year/date/case number and opens a text only for <= 40 candidates (narrow with date_from/date_to); text of pre-2000 decisions is noisy OCR
  • Other courts (justice.public.lu): bot check (FriendlyCaptcha); administrative courts (ja.public.lu) answer 403; the Tribunal d'arrondissement ZIPs on data.public.lu are not wired
  • Consolidated text of acts outside the ~1,400 consolidated ones: not published online (original Mémorial text served)
