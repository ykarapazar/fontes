Tajikistan: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Tajikistan case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Constitutional Court of the Republic of Tajikistan (constcourt.tj): case law and legislation
  • Official website of the President of the Republic of Tajikistan (president.tj): legislation
  • Базаи қонунгузории ҶТ, National Legislation Centre under the President of the Republic of Tajikistan (base.mmk.tj): legislation

What it covers

Constitutional Court decisions (29 acts, ru/tj) and the Constitution plus a few court laws from constcourt.tj; presidential decrees/orders and Government resolutions from president.tj; the Constitution, codes and laws 1993-2019 (621 acts, tj/ru) from the National Legislation Centre's base.mmk.tj, which stopped updating in December 2019. The current legal database (adlia.tj) and the general courts are unreachable from abroad.

What it does not cover

  • Laws and codes adopted or amended after December 2019: base.mmk.tj is frozen at 2019 and adlia.tj, mmk.tj, parlament.tj, majmilli.tj time out from the UK (2026-11-10); minjust.tj certificate expired
  • General and Supreme Court decisions (sud.tj): times out from the UK (2026-11-10)
  • Constitutional Court decisions beyond the 29 posted on constcourt.tj: not published online
  • Ministerial regulations: not researched yet
