Vietnam: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Vietnam case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Bộ Tư pháp: Cơ sở dữ liệu quốc gia về văn bản pháp luật (vbpl.vn): legislation
  • Cổng Thông tin điện tử Chính phủ: Hệ thống văn bản (vanban.chinhphu.vn): legislation
  • Tòa án nhân dân tối cao: Trang tin điện tử về án lệ (anle.toaan.gov.vn): case law

What it covers

Legislation: the Ministry of Justice database vbpl.vn (all constitutions, codes, laws, ordinances, resolutions and lower central texts, HTML as issued; consolidated texts are separate documents), searched by URL slug; filter source=chinhphu for the Government Portal PDFs. Case law: only the Supreme People's Court precedents (án lệ, ~90), searched by number, title and field; the 2026 ones are scans and can't be read. Published judgments (congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn) are unreachable. Vietnamese only.

What it does not cover

  • Full-text / accented search on vbpl.vn: the site's search runs through /api/, which robots.txt disallows, so search matches the sitemap URL slugs (words of the title without diacritics, plus the number)
  • Consolidated constitution: vbpl.vn has the 2013 Constitution as issued and the 2025 amending resolution 203/2025/QH15 separately, no consolidated text
  • Possibly missing laws: gaps in the vbpl.vn law-number sequences (checked 2026-11-10): QH12 no. 4, QH13 nos. 25 and 34, QH15 no. 115, QH16 nos. 13, 14, 17, 19-21 (not verified that these numbers are laws); completeness before 2002 not verified; documents published only as a file have no text
  • Published court judgments (congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn): connection reset / timed out from the UK (2026-11-06)
  • Codification (phapdien.moj.gov.vn): timed out from the UK
  • vanban.chinhphu.vn scanned PDFs (e.g. its copy of the 2015 Civil Code; vbpl.vn has the text) and the 2026 precedents 83-90: scans only, no text
