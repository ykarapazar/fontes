Brazil: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Brazil case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • STF – Pesquisa de Jurisprudência (Supremo Tribunal Federal): case law
  • normas.leg.br (Senado Federal / Câmara dos Deputados) – consolidated federal legislation: legislation

What it covers

Case law: STF only (acórdãos, monocratic decisions, súmulas). Legislation: federal norms consolidated per article from normas.leg.br; ids are LexML URNs (aliases cc, cf, cpc, cp, cpp, cdc, clt, ctn). For cp, cpp, cdc, clt and ctn normas.leg.br has only metadata, so their current text comes from the Câmara compilation on legis.senado.leg.br (flat text, no per-version history).

What it does not cover

  • Superior Tribunal de Justiça (STJ): SCON is behind a Cloudflare JS challenge (not touched). Open-data portal (CC-BY) not built: text ZIPs are partial (2026-10-09: 100 texts for 1,293 listed decisions), one file per publication day, no index, robots Crawl-Delay 10
  • Other federal and state courts: CNJ DataJud is metadata only and timed out from the UK; court sites not researched yet
  • Norms with metadata only in normas.leg.br: text is taken from the Senado-hosted Câmara compilation when it exists; Planalto (usual consolidated text) refuses connections from abroad
  • State and municipal legislation: not researched yet

Past versions

get_law_on_date and get_law_history read Lexifina's record (not the official publisher).
