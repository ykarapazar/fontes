Cuba: legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Cuba legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Gaceta Oficial de la República de Cuba (gacetaoficial.gob.cu): legislation
  • Ministerio de Justicia de Cuba (Gaceta Oficial reposts): legislation

What it covers

Legislation only: the Gaceta Oficial's own norm database and issue PDFs (as published, not consolidated; text layer), plus MINJUS reposts. Id 'Constitución' = the 2019 Constitution as reformed and republished whole in 2025; 'Código Civil' = the site's 2022 updated edition (later amendments not applied). The Gazette site obeys a 10 s crawl delay and often times out from abroad. No case law source.

What it does not cover

  • Case law: Tribunal Supremo terms forbid access 'utilizando medios automatizados'; it has no decision database anyway
  • Primary acts: the Gazette's norm database indexes ~103 Leyes and ~340 Decretos-Leyes; older acts appear mostly as later republications, so completeness for pre-2019 in-force acts is unverified (2026-11-10: Leyes 75, 76, 85, 113, 65 are indexed; Ley 41/1983 and Ley 51/1985 are not, and the site's issue archive starts in 1990, so pre-1990 acts never republished are not online). Ley 59/1987 Código Civil is not indexed (served from the site's 2022 updated edition, id 'Código Civil'); Código Penal Ley 151/2022 and Código de las Familias Ley 156/2022 are indexed
  • Consolidation: texts as published, amendments not applied (except the Constitution's 2025 republication)
  • Norms without a code (mostly pre-2019) open as their whole Gazette issue, not split out
