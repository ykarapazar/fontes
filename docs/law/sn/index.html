Senegal: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Senegal case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Conseil constitutionnel du Sénégal (decisions and consolidated Constitution): case law and legislation
  • Primature / Secrétariat général du Gouvernement (lois, décrets, codes): legislation
  • Ministère de la Justice du Sénégal (codes, lois and décrets as PDF): legislation
  • JURICAF (AHJUCAF): Cour suprême decisions (unofficial): case law (unofficial; every answer says so)

What it covers

Case law: Conseil constitutionnel (41 decisions, most scanned PDFs without text); Cour suprême only through the unofficial JURICAF database (filter court=supreme, first 10 hits, no paging; get_decision by JURICAF id works). No appeal-court or first-instance (tribunal du travail) decisions. Journal officiel site unreachable (2026-11). Legislation: Constitution (consolidated to 2024-02), ~60 laws, decrees and codes from primature.sn, and ~60 PDF texts from the Ministry of Justice (Code pénal, CPP, COCC, Code de la famille, Code du travail, OHADA acts). Not every law: no Journal officiel online.

What it does not cover

  • Cour suprême: official site coursupreme.gouv.sn is a maintenance page (2026-11-10). JURICAF (unofficial, ODbL) holds ~7,458 decisions to 2025-04, but its robots.txt disallows ?page=, so search shows 10 hits per query and the archive cannot be listed or searched exhaustively
  • Appeal courts (cours d'appel) and first-instance courts / tribunaux du travail: no decisions in any source here; Cour suprême decisions that review them can be read, the lower judgment cannot
  • Journal officiel: www.jo.gouv.sn connection timeout on http and https (2026-11-05/06 and 2026-11-10); jo.gouv.sn NXDOMAIN
  • Statutes not complete: no official full set of laws online; primature.sn and the Ministry of Justice publish a selection (~120 texts), as published, not consolidated
  • Most Conseil constitutionnel decisions (2023-2026 files): scans only, no text
  • Conseil constitutionnel decisions before 2019 and from 2020-2021: not published online (41 decisions)
  • Codes (18 on primature.sn): PDF attachments answered 404 (2026-11-06); the Ministry of Justice PDFs cover the penal, criminal procedure, civil/commercial (COCC), family and labour codes
  • Consolidated laws and Constitution changes after 2024-02: not published online; sgg.sn has TLS errors
