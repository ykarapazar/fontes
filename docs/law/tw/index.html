Fontes / Coverage by jurisdiction

Taiwan: case law for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Taiwan case law at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Judicial Yuan 裁判書查詢 (FJUD, judgment.judicial.gov.tw): case law
  • Ministry of Justice 全國法規資料庫 (law.moj.gov.tw, LawAll.aspx only): legislation

What it covers

Case law: all FJUD courts (not the Constitutional Court) via keyword search (supports 台北地院105訴123-style case numbers and & + - operators). Statutes by MOJ pcode only (B0000001 民法, C0000001 刑法, N0030001 勞動基準法, A0000001 憲法; A0000001 is the 1947 base text only: Articles 25-34 (incl. 27, National Assembly) are suspended by 增修條文 A0000002): no law search, robots.txt allows only LawAll.aspx. Constitutional Court site unreachable from outside Taiwan (2026-11).

What it does not cover

  • Legislation search, per-article pages and the English site (law.moj.gov.tw): robots.txt allows only LawAll.aspx
  • Constitutional Court (cons.judicial.gov.tw): timed out from the UK (2026-11-05)
  • Judicial Yuan open data (opendata/data.judicial.gov.tw): timed out from the UK (2026-11-05)
  • Local government regulations: not researched yet
