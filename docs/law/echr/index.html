European Court of Human Rights: case law for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search European Court of Human Rights case law at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • HUDOC (European Court of Human Rights): case law
  • European Convention on Human Rights and Protocols (official PDF, read it there; not available through this server): legislation

What it covers

Case law only (ECtHR judgments, decisions, communicated cases, advisory opinions, Commission decisions) via HUDOC. Ids are HUDOC itemids; ECLI and application numbers also work. Non-commercial reuse only. Convention and Protocol text is not available here (search_legislation is unsupported): read it as a PDF at https://www.echr.coe.int/documents/d/echr/convention_eng

What it does not cover

  • Convention and Protocols text: only a PDF on echr.coe.int; coe.int treaty office blocks our client (Cloudflare)
  • Press releases: in HUDOC but filtered out of search; not built
  • Execution of judgments (Committee of Ministers, HUDOC-EXEC): not researched yet
  • Parties' pleadings and case files: not researched yet

Questions to try

Ask in any language; the search words work best in the language of the source.

  • Find European Court of Human Rights case law on bulk interception

Install

Claude Code: claude mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Codex: codex mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Claude Desktop and one-click installers for Mac and Windows: the latest release. Other apps: the installation guide.

Jurisdiction code: echr. Whether each source is working today: source status, checked daily.

Fontes is a research aid, not legal advice. Read the full decision or provision at the source before you rely on it.
