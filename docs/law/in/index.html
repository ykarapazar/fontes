India: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search India case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • India Code (Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice; run by NIC): legislation (off unless you hold the publisher's permission)
  • Indian Supreme Court Judgments (AWS Open Data; Dattam Labs mirror of the Supreme Court's e-SCR): case law (unofficial; every answer says so)

What it covers

Central and state Acts from India Code, section by section (off unless WLM_PERMITTED). Law ids are India Code act ids (AC_CEN_...), or '9 of 1872' for central Acts. Supreme Court: S.C.R.-reported judgments 1950 to date from an unofficial AWS Open Data mirror of e-SCR; search one year at a time (parties, citation, coram, headnote).

What it does not cover

  • Legislation (India Code, all Acts): terms forbid automated use ('Not use any automated means'); needs written permission
  • Constitution: legislative.gov.in (official PDFs) answers our User-Agent with an Akamai 403; Constitute Project text is non-commercial and flags a 2024 amendment not incorporated; not built
  • Supreme Court, unreported judgments: not in e-SCR or the AWS mirror; official sci.gov.in judgment search is behind a CAPTCHA
  • Supreme Court full-text search: the mirror has no search index; search matches parties, citations, coram and headnote within one year
  • High Courts (AWS Open Data, unofficial, CC BY 4.0): source exists, not built
  • Schedules of Acts: not published online as text in India Code's API (SCHEDULE items carry no text)
  • Subordinate legislation (rules, regulations, notifications): not researched yet

Questions to try

Ask in any language; the search words work best in the language of the source.

  • Find India case law on Kesavananda Bharati 1973
  • Find India legislation: Indian Contract Act
