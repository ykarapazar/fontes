Bosnia: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Bosnia and Herzegovina case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Ustavni sud Bosne i Hercegovine (ustavnisud.ba) – decisions database: case law
  • Vlada Federacije BiH (fbihvlada.gov.ba) – Hronološki registar zakona (Federation laws as published): legislation
  • Parlamentarna skupština BiH (parlament.ba) – Ustav BiH and Prečišćeni tekstovi zakona (state level): legislation

What it covers

Case law: Constitutional Court of BiH decisions (1997-, full text; English for leading cases). Legislation: the Constitution of BiH (with Amandman I), the Parliament's unofficial consolidated state acts (a selection) and Federation of BiH laws as published (1996-). Ordinary courts' case law and the state/RS gazettes are paid. Provisions of the Constitution are returned as whole articles ('II/3' gives Član II).

What it does not cover

  • Ordinary courts' case law (csd.pravosudje.ba): needs registration and is paid
  • State-level acts: only the Parliament's selection of unofficial consolidated texts (135 entries, 81 distinct acts) is reached; the full set of acts as adopted sits behind /olaw/OLawDetails* (robots disallow) and Službeni glasnik BiH (paid)
  • Republika Srpska laws: Službeni glasnik RS (slglasnik.org) is paid
  • Brčko District laws (skupstinabd.ba): free, robots allow; source exists but not built yet
  • Consolidated texts: no free official source found (FBiH laws as published only; paragraf.ba is commercial)

Past versions

get_law_on_date and get_law_history read Lexifina's record (not the official publisher).
