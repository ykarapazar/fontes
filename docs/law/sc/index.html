Seychelles: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Seychelles case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Judiciary of Seychelles website (judiciary.sc): Decisions from the Courts: case law
  • Official Gazette of Seychelles (gazette.sc): Acts, S.I.s and Bills: legislation
  • Judiciary of Seychelles website (judiciary.sc): Laws of Seychelles (Constitution and main codes): legislation

What it covers

Case law: Supreme Court and Constitutional Court decisions listed on judiciary.sc, 2025 on (Court of Appeal list empty). Legislation: the Constitution (revised to 2018) and the Penal, Civil, Criminal Procedure and Civil Procedure Codes as posted on judiciary.sc; Acts, S.I.s and Bills as gazetted, 2020 on, not consolidated.

What it does not cover

  • Court of Appeal decisions: judiciary.sc's Court of Appeal table is empty; not published online
  • Judgments before 2025: not listed on judiciary.sc (SeyLII is non-official and its terms prohibit scraping)
  • Acts before 2020 other than the Constitution and the four codes on judiciary.sc: not published by an official host (legalaffairs.gov.sc does not resolve); SeyLII has consolidated Acts but its terms say 'Scraping and bulk downloading from this website is strictly prohibited', so it is not used
  • Constitution amendments after August 2018: not merged into the judiciary.sc text; the amendment Acts from 2020 are on gazette.sc
  • Employment Act, Companies Act and other principal Acts before 2020: not on any official host, so a title search for them finds only later amendments, regulations and Bills
  • Gazette items before 2020: not published online (paid request by email only)
  • Judgment PDFs with no text layer: scans only, no text

Past versions
