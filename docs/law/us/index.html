United States: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search United States case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • govinfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office): USCOURTS opinions and U.S. Reports: case law and legislation
  • Supreme Court of the United States: opinions of the Court (supremecourt.gov): case law
  • National Archives: Constitution of the United States (transcription): legislation
  • govinfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office): Statutes at Large and Public and Private Laws: legislation
  • United States Code (Office of the Law Revision Counsel, U.S. House): legislation

What it covers

Federal only. Case law: govinfo (lower federal courts ~2004 on + bound U.S. Reports to vol. 586) and supremecourt.gov (Supreme Court opinions from October Term 2018 on: collection SUPREMECOURT; ids '606 U.S. 942' or a docket), PDF text. Legislation: US Code by title and section, e.g. '42 USC 1983', and the Constitution ('US Constitution'; provisions 'preamble', 'art. I', 'art. I sec. 8', 'amend. XIV') from the National Archives transcription. Acts as enacted: Statutes at Large vols 1-138 (1789-2024; ids '1 Stat. 50', 'STATUTE-1-Pg50', 'Pub. L. 85-1'; provisions are Stat. pages) and public/private laws from 1995 ('Pub. L. 119-1'; provisions are sections); full-text search with filter collection STATUTE or PLAW. No state law, no CFR.

What it does not cover

  • Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR): robots.txt disallows the full-text endpoint /api/versioner/v1/full/
  • Supreme Court from October Term 2018 on: searchable by name, docket, citation and the Court's one-line summary only (no full-text search); 'opinions relating to orders' not wired
  • Lower federal court opinions before ~2004: not in govinfo USCOURTS
  • State statutes and state case law: not built yet (federal only)
  • Federal Register and Congress bills: source exists and works but not built yet
  • US Constitution: National Archives transcription of the parchment text (original spelling; superseded clauses are not marked in the articles, only in amendment notes)
  • Statutes at Large: page PDFs with GPO's text layer; older volumes are scans, so expect OCR errors, and a granule's first and last pages can carry the neighbouring acts. Laws newer than vol. 138 (2024) come from PLAW (1995 on) until bound
  • Public laws: as enacted, not consolidated (the consolidated text of codified provisions is the US Code); new laws reach govinfo PLAW some weeks after enactment
  • Case-law search beyond ~10/day: needs a free api.data.gov key (WLM_US_GOVINFO_KEY), else DEMO_KEY
