Suriname: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Suriname case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Rechtspraak Suriname, Hof van Justitie (rechtspraak.sr): case law
  • Surinaamse wetten, De Nationale Assemblee (dna.sr): legislation

What it covers

Dutch only. Case law: anonymised decisions of the Hof van Justitie, kantongerechten and disciplinary boards from rechtspraak.sr (about 1,400; full-text search, at most 500 hits shown). Legislation: laws published by the National Assembly (dna.sr) as PDFs. Title search only; 'geldende teksten t/m 2005' are pre-2005 consolidations, with later changes listed separately (mostly scanned PDFs that cannot be read here). dna.sr terms allow copying parts with a link to the source; commercial use needs written permission.

What it does not cover

  • Consolidated texts after 2005: 'geldende teksten t/m 2005' are older consolidations; later changes are separate laws
  • Amendment laws ('wijzigingen na 2005', e.g. the 2022 Burgerlijk Wetboek amendment, the 2023 Grondwet amendment) and some other laws are scanned PDFs without a text layer: they cannot be read here; open the PDF on dna.sr (the error gives its link). Laws with a poor OCR text layer can lack some articles
  • Legislation full-text search: dna.sr is searched by title words only; not built yet
  • Case law beyond 500 hits per search: rechtspraak.sr shows at most 500
  • Decisions outside the court's selection: not published online (selection and anonymisation decree)
  • Official gazette and subordinate regulations: not researched yet
