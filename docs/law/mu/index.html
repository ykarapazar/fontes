Mauritius: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Mauritius case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Find Case Law, The National Archives (UK): Judicial Committee of the Privy Council judgments: case law
  • Supreme Court of Mauritius website (supremecourt.govmu.org): judgment search (subordinate courts) and legislation library: case law and legislation (off unless you hold the publisher's permission)
  • Revised Laws of Mauritius, Attorney General's Office (attorneygeneral.govmu.org): legislation
  • National Assembly of Mauritius (mauritiusassembly.govmu.org): Acts as passed: legislation

What it covers

Case law: Privy Council (final court) judgments in Mauritian appeals, Nov 2009 on, full text, from Find Case Law (UK National Archives); subordinate courts (Intermediate, Industrial, District, Rodrigues, Children's Court), 2020 on, OFF by default (the source's robots.txt disallows all automated access since 2026-11-10; court=intermediate etc. fail unless the operator holds the Supreme Court's permission); no Supreme Court (SCJ) judgments are published online by an allowed source. Legislation: the Constitution and ~660 Acts as consolidated in the Revised Laws of Mauritius (Attorney General's Office; updated to June 2017, some later issues); Acts as passed 2009-2023 (National Assembly; 2018 from the AG); Acts, GNs, Bills, Proclamations and Reprints as enacted, 2024 on (Supreme Court library). Amendments after a Revised Laws issue are not merged: read the later amending Acts.

What it does not cover

  • Privy Council judgments before Nov 2009: only on BAILII (no API, forbids scraping); Find Case Law's UKPC collection starts 2009
  • Privy Council country filter is heuristic (Find Case Law has no country field): title '(Mauritius)' or header 'From the Supreme Court of Mauritius'; a judgment with neither is missed (e.g. [2016] UKPC 30, [2015] UKPC 14 'From the Court of Appeal of Mauritius')
  • Supreme Court (SCJ) judgments: not published in the site's judgment collection (checked 2026-11-06)
  • Subordinate-court judgments before 2020: not published online on the site
  • Consolidated Acts: the AG's Revised Laws stop at 30 June 2017 for most Acts (a few later issues); later amendments are only in the amending Acts as passed; no official up-to-date consolidation online. Whether the AG's A-Z list holds every Act in force is unverified (~660 titles); title search only (AG search API returns 500)
  • Acts as passed: the National Assembly tables miss some PDFs (rows without links); 2018 Acts from the AG list have no titles (number only); Acts as passed before 2009 not online (their text is in the Revised Laws)
  • MauritiusLII judgments: robots.txt disallows /judgments/ and sets Content-Signal ai-input=no
