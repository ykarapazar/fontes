Israel: legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Israel legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Knesset OData (ParliamentInfo) – Israeli law database (official): legislation
  • Sefer HaChukim (ספר החוקים, Reshumot) as-published PDFs on fs.knesset.gov.il (official): legislation
  • Open Law Book (ספר החוקים הפתוח), Hebrew Wikisource – non-official (crowd-maintained, Wikimedia Foundation): legislation (unofficial; every answer says so)

What it covers

Legislation only, in Hebrew: Knesset law database (official metadata, id = IsraelLawID) + consolidated text from the non-official Open Law Book on Wikisource + official as-published text from the Sefer HaChukim PDFs (ids '<IsraelLawID>/original' and '<IsraelLawID>/<BillID>' for each amending law). No case law: the court sites block automated access and require a signed undertaking.

What it does not cover

  • Case law (Supreme Court sites): blocks our client (unauthorised-activity page); bulk access needs permission (signed undertaking)
  • Official consolidated text: not published online free (Nevo/Takdin are paid); the consolidated text here is non-official Wikisource; the official text is per publication (original law + each amending law), except the few laws the Knesset re-published as נוסח משולב
  • Official gazette text quality: read from the PDF text layer; pre-~2005 scans are OCR (about 1-5% misread words in samples), and later born-digital PDFs can misplace numbers, punctuation and margin headings; sections are not split (pages only)
  • Mandate-era ordinances (פקודה): the official PDF is the Dinei Medinat Israel [נוסח חדש] republication where the Knesset database holds it (KNS_DocumentLaw); scanned OCR, same quality caveats
  • Search covers law names only (Knesset OData), not the text; regulations (תקנות, Kovetz HaTakanot on gov.il, Cloudflare-walled): not covered
