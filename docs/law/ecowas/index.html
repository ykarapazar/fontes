ECOWAS Community Court of Justice: case law for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search ECOWAS Community Court of Justice case law at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • ECOWAS Community Court of Justice website (courtecowas.org): case law
  • ECOWAS Court Law Reports (CCJELR), English volumes 2004-2017 (courtecowas.org): case law

What it covers

ECOWAS Court of Justice decisions: the official Law Reports 2004-2017 (English, full text; search with report_year) and the Court's online list (about 210, mostly 2015 onwards, English with some French/Portuguese; about half are scans without text).

What it does not cover

  • Text of decisions from 2018 on that are only scans (about half of the online list): no text layer (no OCR); Law Reports after 2017 are not online (2021-2024 volumes in preparation, Court news 2025)
  • Decisions 2004-2017 not selected for the Law Reports and missing from the online list: not published online
  • Case files and pending-case documents: the Court's per-case pages are empty (not published online)
  • ECOWAS treaties, protocols and Community acts: not available here (no legislation source). The Court's Basic Texts page, https://courtecowas.org/about_us/basic-text/, links the Revised ECOWAS Treaty, the Court Protocol A/P1/7/91 and Supplementary Protocol A/SP.1/01/05 as PDFs; the Community acts are in the ECOWAS Official Journal at https://www.ecowas.int
