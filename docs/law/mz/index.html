Mozambique: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Mozambique case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Tribunal Supremo (ts.gov.mz): judgments tables of the TS and the Tribunais Superiores de Recurso, and its legislation page: case law and legislation
  • Conselho Constitucional (cconstitucional.org.mz): acórdãos and deliberações: case law

What it covers

Case law: Tribunal Supremo, the three Tribunais Superiores de Recurso (~930 judgments) and the Conselho Constitucional (~290). Legislation: ~110 PDFs the Tribunal Supremo hosts (Constitution, key laws, decrees), as published, not consolidated. Portuguese.

What it does not cover

  • Consolidated legislation (MozLex, mozlex.ts.gov.mz): robots.txt bans it ('recolha automatizada ... nao e autorizada')
  • Case-law database (mozjuris.ts.gov.mz, incl. Administrative Tribunal): Anubis bot challenge blocks our client
  • Boletim da República (inm.gov.mz): PDFs need an account; terms bar copying ('É proibida a cópia') without authorisation
  • Scanned PDFs (about half of the CC decisions checked, some laws): scans only, no text
  • Provincial and district courts: not researched yet
  • Assembleia da República (parlamento.mz): site down (parked domain, 2026-11-06)
