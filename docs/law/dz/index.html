Algeria: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Algeria case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Journal Officiel de la République Algérienne (JORADP), Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement: legislation
  • Cour constitutionnelle (and former Conseil constitutionnel) of Algeria: case law

What it covers

Legislation: Journal Officiel index since 1962, texts as published (not consolidated) in the French edition (official translation; Arabic is authoritative), full text only for issues from 2002 (older ones are scans). Law id 'Constitution': the 2020 text with later revision laws applied. Keywords must be JORADP lexicon words. Case law: decisions and opinions (avis) of the Constitutional Court and the former Constitutional Council, 1989 onward (French edition; Arabic is authoritative). No Supreme Court or Council of State case law (coursupreme.dz did not respond).

What it does not cover

  • Supreme Court case law: coursupreme.dz connect timeout on http and https, www and bare (2026-11-05/06 and again 2026-11-10)
  • Council of State and lower-court decisions: not researched yet
  • Tribunal des conflits, administrative courts of appeal and military courts: not researched yet
  • Journal Officiel texts before 2002: scans only, no text layer and no OCR here (reference returned only; Arabic issues are scans too, 1990 and 2000 checked). Of the primary acts in the JO index (checked 2026-11-10), 1,794 date from before 2002 (Loi 399, Loi organique 3, Ordonnance 1,354, Décret législatif 38) against 475 with text from 2002 on; base texts of codes still in force such as the Code civil (Ord. 75-58), Code pénal (Ord. 66-156) and Code de la famille (Loi 84-11) are scan-only, their post-2002 amending acts are readable
  • Arabic (authoritative) JO issues: /FTP/jo-arabe/{year}/A{year}{jo:03}.pdf, HTTP 200; 1975, 1990, 2000 and 2001 have no text layer; where a text layer exists its font map is broken differently per era (2003: custom-encoded glyph garbage; 2008: letters in order but words reversed and ligatures mapped to 'J', '~', 's'; 2024: each line character-reversed, digits scrambled), so the Arabic text is not served (checked 2026-11-10)
  • Laws are as published, not consolidated (no official consolidation on joradp.dz; mjustice.dz, droit.mjustice.dz and apn.dz timed out from the UK, 2026-11-10)
  • Arabic (authoritative) edition of laws and Constitutional Court decisions: source has it, not built (French only)
