Côte d'Ivoire: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Côte d'Ivoire case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Cour de cassation de Côte d'Ivoire: jurisprudence (eservices / sigcc): case law
  • Assemblée nationale de Côte d'Ivoire: lois (assnat.ci): legislation

What it covers

Cour de cassation judgments (~100, 2023-2024, PDF full text, anonymised; keyword search covers the subject line only; most are anonymised, but some copies name the parties in full) and ~135 laws listed by the Assemblée nationale (as adopted, mostly 2012-2020). French.

What it does not cover

  • Cour de cassation judgments outside 2023-2024: not published online (~100 judgments in all)
  • Journal officiel and Cour suprême (sgg.gouv.ci, coursupreme.ci): TLS certificate expired
  • Conseil constitutionnel decisions: robots allows (Crawl-delay 10), PDFs; source exists but not built yet
  • Decrees, ordinances, consolidated texts and laws outside ~2012-2020: not on assnat.ci; JO site cert expired (above)
  • Full-text case-law search: the Cour de cassation search matches the subject line only

Past versions

get_law_on_date and get_law_history read Lexifina's record (not the official publisher).
