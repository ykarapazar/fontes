Canada: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Canada case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Supreme Court of Canada judgments (decisions.scc-csc.ca, hosted by Lexum for the Court): case law
  • Justice Laws Website, Department of Justice Canada: legislation

What it covers

Federal only. Case law = Supreme Court of Canada (Lexum); ids are Lexum item numbers, citations like '2024 SCC 10' also work. Legislation = Justice Laws consolidated Acts and regulations; ids like 'C-46' (Criminal Code), provisions like 'section-718' or 's. 718'. English text; French at the same sites (/fr/, /fra/). No provincial law, no lower courts.

What it does not cover

  • Provincial and territorial legislation: BC Laws API works (not built yet); Ontario e-Laws is a JS app; rest not researched
  • Federal Court, Federal Court of Appeal, Tax Court and tribunals: not researched yet
  • Provincial courts: CanLII terms prohibit scraping and it serves a JS challenge; official court sites not researched yet
  • Constitution Acts 1867-1982: served (law id 'Const'); endnotes (amendment history) left out, page states no 'current to' date; other constitutional instruments (Manitoba Act, Constitution Act 1930 etc.) only in the 1990 /eng/ConstRpt/ report, not built
  • Federal legislation search is by title only (no full-text search over section text)
  • French text: same sites at /fr/ and /fra/; source exists but not built yet

Questions to try

Ask in any language; the search words work best in the language of the source.

Install

Claude Code: claude mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Codex: codex mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Claude Desktop and one-click installers for Mac and Windows: the latest release. Other apps: the installation guide.
