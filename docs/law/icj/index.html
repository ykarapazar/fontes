International Court of Justice (ICJ): case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search International Court of Justice (ICJ) case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • International Court of Justice website (www.icj-cij.org): case pages and document search: case law and legislation

What it covers

ICJ judgments, advisory opinions and orders: search the Court's document index and open case records (case number, e.g. 178) or a document's details; also the Statute and the Rules of Court (ids 'statute', 'rules'). Texts are PDF-only behind a bot challenge, so no full text: metadata and PDF links. Non-commercial use only.

What it does not cover

  • Full text of judgments, advisory opinions and orders: bot check / blocks our client (Cloudflare on every PDF); metadata and links only
  • Pleadings, written and oral proceedings texts: bot check / blocks our client (same Cloudflare PDF block)
  • Legislation: only the Statute of the Court and the Rules of Court (1978, as amended) from the Court's own pages, nothing else (Practice Directions are not included)
  • Permanent Court of International Justice (1922-1946) records: not researched yet

Questions to try

Ask in any language; the search words work best in the language of the source.

  • Find International Court of Justice (ICJ) case law on genocide
  • Find International Court of Justice (ICJ) legislation: statute

Install

Claude Code: claude mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Codex: codex mcp add fontes -- uvx fontes-mcp. Claude Desktop and one-click installers for Mac and Windows: the latest release. Other apps: the installation guide.
