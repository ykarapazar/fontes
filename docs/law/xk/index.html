Kosovo: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Kosovo case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Gjykata Kushtetuese e Republikës së Kosovës (Constitutional Court), gjk-ks.org: case law
  • Gazeta Zyrtare e Republikës së Kosovës (Official Gazette, Office of the Prime Minister), gzk.rks-gov.net: legislation

What it covers

Case law: Constitutional Court only (2009-), sq/en/sr. Legislation: Official Gazette, Albanian: Constitution, codes and laws as published; the Gazette's consolidated versions are separate acts titled '(KONSOLIDUAR)'.

What it does not cover

  • Ordinary-court case law (gjyqesori-rks.org): 403 to our client; robots.txt sets Content-Signal ai-train=no
  • Consolidated laws: the Gazette has acts as published; it has consolidated only some laws (158 '(KONSOLIDUAR)' acts on 2026-11-10, all reachable: search 'konsoliduar'); for the rest read the act with its amending acts
  • Serbian and English texts of legislation: the site's language is a per-session switch; not built yet
  • Old Constitutional Court documents with no text in any language: scans only, no text
  • Kosovo Specialist Chambers (repository.scp-ks.org): not built; its legal notice allows reuse only for private, information or education use and asks written permission for anything else

Past versions

get_law_on_date and get_law_history read Lexifina's record (not the official publisher).
