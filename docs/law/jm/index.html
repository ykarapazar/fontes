Jamaica: case law for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Jamaica case law at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • Find Case Law, The National Archives (UK): Judicial Committee of the Privy Council judgments: case law
  • Supreme Court of Jamaica judgments (supremecourt.gov.jm): case law
  • Court of Appeal of Jamaica judgments (courtofappeal.gov.jm): case law

What it covers

Privy Council (final court) judgments in Jamaican appeals, Nov 2009 on, full-text search, from Find Case Law (UK National Archives). Supreme Court and Court of Appeal judgments from the courts' own sites (mostly 1990s-; older PDFs are often scans without text). Search matches party names / title and neutral citation, not full text. No legislation: the Laws of Jamaica site (laws.moj.gov.jm) is down for maintenance (HTTP 503, 2026-11-10).

What it does not cover

  • Privy Council judgments before Nov 2009: only on BAILII (no API, forbids scraping); Find Case Law's UKPC collection starts 2009
  • Privy Council country filter is heuristic (Find Case Law has no country field): title '(Jamaica)' or header 'From the Court of Appeal of Jamaica'; a judgment with neither is missed (e.g. [2017] UKPC 21, header 'From the Court of Appeal Jamaica')
  • Consolidated legislation and the Constitution (Laws of Jamaica, laws.moj.gov.jm): site down, HTTP 503 'Website Maintenance Notice' pointing to LIPJ (http and https, 2026-11-10)
  • LIPJ portal (lipj.gov.jm, ~18k acts/regulations): needs permission (terms §3 'lawful, non-commercial purposes'; §6 no exploiting portal functionality without prior written consent)
  • As-passed Acts PDFs (japarliament.gov.jm): source exists but not built yet (no consolidated text, Crawl-delay 15)
  • Older judgments: scans only, no text (no OCR)
  • Parish and other lower courts: not researched yet
