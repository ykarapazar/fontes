Czechia: case law and legislation for your AI app

Fontes lets your AI app search Czechia case law and legislation at the source and link every result to the original. It is a free, open-source MCP server for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and other apps.

Sources

  • rozhodnuti.justice.cz (Ministry of Justice): case law
  • e-Sbírka (Ministry of the Interior): legislation
  • Sbírka soudních rozhodnutí a stanovisek (Nejvyšší soud), sbirka.nsoud.cz: case law
  • Vyhledávač NSS (Nejvyšší správní soud), vyhledavac.nssoud.cz: case law

What it covers

Case law: by default district, regional and high courts (rozhodnuti.justice.cz, since 2020, anonymised). court='NS' searches the Supreme Court's official Sbírka (1919-, ids 'sbirka:25671'); court='NSS' searches the Supreme Administrative Court (ids 'nssoud:786898', the Vyhledávač NSS document id; court='SPRAVNI' adds regional administrative decisions). ECLI:CZ:NS: and ECLI:CZ:NSS: ids resolve through those sources. Laws: cite as '89/2012 Sb.', provisions as '§ 2'.

What it does not cover

  • Supreme Administrative Court (NSS): regional administrative decisions there (court 'SPRAVNI') are whatever the NSS database holds, completeness unverified
  • Constitutional Court (ÚS): nalus.usoud.cz works (HTML search) but not built yet
  • Supreme Court (NS): only decisions published in the Sbírka are reachable; the full decision database rozhodnuti.nsoud.cz has robots.txt 'User-agent: * Disallow: /' (only DG_JUSTICE_CRAWLER allowed), so it is not used
  • Lower-court decisions before 2020: rozhodnuti.justice.cz starts in 2020; older sources not researched yet
  • Regional and municipal regulations: not researched yet
