# Contributing to Fontes

Thank you for wanting to help. Fontes is maintained by one person, and for now it does **not
accept outside code contributions**: pull requests will be closed without review. This keeps
every adapter's source checks (terms, robots.txt, rate limits) in one pair of hands.

What helps most, and is very welcome:

- **Report a broken or wrong source.** A court site changed, a search returns nothing, a text
  is out of date, or a result says "official" when it is not. Use the
  [Broken or wrong source](https://github.com/ykarapazar/fontes/issues/new?template=source.yml)
  form, and link the official page that shows the right text.
- **Request a jurisdiction** or a missing court or legislation type, with the official website
  if you know it: [Request a jurisdiction](https://github.com/ykarapazar/fontes/issues/new?template=jurisdiction.yml).
  [docs/COVERAGE.md](docs/COVERAGE.md) already says, for each jurisdiction, what is not
  reached and why; please check it first.
- **Report a bug** in Fontes itself (install, errors, crashes):
  [Bug report](https://github.com/ykarapazar/fontes/issues/new?template=bug.yml). You can also
  ask your AI app to "report a Fontes bug" or run `fontes report-bug`; you see the report before
  anything is sent.

Please never put client details or other confidential facts in an issue. Issues are public.

**If you run one of the sources** and something is wrong (your terms, your robots.txt, your rate
limits), email fontesmcp@proton.me. The source will be fixed or switched off.

Security problems: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); please do not open a public issue.

Everyone taking part is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Questions: [docs/FAQ.md](docs/FAQ.md), or fontesmcp@proton.me.
