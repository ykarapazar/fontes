**Verification notation**

- **VERIFIED** means the stated page, document, or API response was opened successfully on **2026-11-06**. “Index only” does not establish full-text access.
- **UNVERIFIED** means working document access was not established. **Prior** findings come from the repository’s November 5–6 research and are explicitly distinguished from fresh checks.
- Unless specified, collection size and complete year range are **unknown**, no public API was verified, and no reuse terms could be located or read. Missing terms or a missing `robots.txt` are **not affirmative permission**.
- Timeouts and certificate failures are reported as observed; they do not establish geo-blocking or frequent downtime.

**Shared Eastern Caribbean case-law source**

The [Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court judgments portal](https://www.eccourts.org/judgments?type=judgments) is **official** and is the strongest shared lead for the six jurisdictions below: High Court and Court of Appeal judgments, English. Country-specific totals and complete historical ranges were not established. A [2025 court procurement notice](https://cms.eccourts.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Judgments-Search-AI-ECSC-RFP-Oct-2025.pdf) describes an approximately 9,000-judgment corpus; that is a publisher estimate, not a measured current count.

- **Machine access:** the current frontend is JavaScript. Its published bundle identifies `https://api.eccourts.org/v1/judgments/list`, `/v1/judgments/{id}` and POST `https://api.eccourts.org/judgments/search`. The listing request **timed out**; the search request/schema was not exercised. The older CMS [WordPress posts search](https://cms.eccourts.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?search=judgment&per_page=1) returned JSON, but the result was **news**, so this does not verify judgment search. Documents are linked from `cms.eccourts.org/wp-content/uploads/…`.
- **Terms:** no substantive reuse conditions located; no quotation available.
- **Robots:** [main site](https://www.eccourts.org/robots.txt): `User-agent: *`, empty `Disallow:`. [CMS](https://cms.eccourts.org/robots.txt) and [API](https://api.eccourts.org/robots.txt): **404**.
- **Status:** **VERIFIED frontend and CMS JSON; judgment search/full-text retrieval UNVERIFIED.** No public-reading login or bot challenge observed. Do not mistake separate member services for a judgment-access requirement.
